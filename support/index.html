For help, email trlmobilesoftware@gmail.com. Include your device, iOS version and what happened. Never send your password or payment-card details.

Start a match

Sit at opposite short ends of the device. Each player chooses a vehicle and locks it in. Choose one shared arena, then hold both Play controls together for three uninterrupted seconds. Releasing either control resets the hold.

Steer and fire

Hold the curved arrow to stop and turn. Release it to drive forward. Each new press reverses the turning direction. Tap or hold the crosshair to fire. Shots use ammo; gold cases refill it, mint cases repair armor and coral cases replace your weapon with three rounds. Crossing an edge brings you back on the opposite side.

The illustrated guide beside the garage logo offers four playable practice lessons. Pause is available during a match.

What does Season 1 unlock?

One non-consumable purchase permanently unlocks vehicles 3–10 and four additional arenas for both players on the same device. It is not a subscription. The store displays the price for your Apple storefront. Weapon pickups and the tutorial stay free.

Restore a purchase

Use the same Apple Account that made the purchase. In the garage, select locked content to open the Season 1 store, then tap Restore Purchases. An internet connection is needed to purchase or restore. Previously verified access can be retained offline. If a purchase is awaiting approval, access arrives after Apple approves and verifies it.

If restoration still fails, reconnect, reopen the app and try again. Contact support with the purchase date and a description of the problem. Apple manages billing and refund requests at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Audio and interruptions

Open the painted audio control in the garage or pause screen to adjust music, effects and engine sound separately. Check the silent switch, system volume and connected headphones if you hear nothing. Returning from the background leaves combat paused; either player can resume.
