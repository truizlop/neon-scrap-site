Put an iPhone or iPad between you. Each player gets two buttons and a very breakable vehicle. Turn, fire, and chase each other around a comic-book scrapyard.

How to play

Coming soon to the App Store

Two buttons are
all you get.

Choose your rides and an arena. Hold both Play buttons for three seconds to begin.

Hold to turn
Hold the arrow to stop and turn. Release to drive forward. Each new press reverses your turning direction.
Tap to fire
Aim with the whole vehicle. You start with three rounds, so collect ammo and swap weapons as you go.

The edge is not an escape.

Drive off one side and return on the other. Cover blocks shots; ammo, repair kits and new weapons keep the fight moving.

Four playable lessons help you find your feet before a friend finds your weak spot.

Start with a
free scrap.

Junk King, Road Riot and Skyline Scrap are free for both players. Every weapon pickup and all four tutorial lessons are included.
